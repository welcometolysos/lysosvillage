DROP YOUR PHOTOS IN HERE
========================

Two folders:

  old-photos\   ->  Old / archive photos of Lysos, for "Then & Now"
  new-photos\   ->  Any recent photos: churches, village, forest, people

HOW:
  Just drag the photo files into the right folder. That's it.
  Straight off your phone, WhatsApp, camera, anywhere.

DON'T WORRY ABOUT:
  - File size (they can be huge, I shrink them)
  - File names (rename them if you like, but you don't have to)
  - Format (jpg, png, heic, whatever)
  - How many

HELPFUL IF YOU CAN (but not required):
  For OLD photos especially, if you know roughly what/when/where,
  either put it in the filename:
      1950s-main-street.jpg
      grandmother-at-the-fountain-1963.jpg
  or just tell Claude in the chat afterwards.

  For "Then & Now" to work best, old photos of places that still
  exist today are gold - the church, the fountain, the square, the
  main street - because I can pair them with a photo of the same
  spot now.

WHEN DONE:
  Tell Claude "photos are in" and I'll take it from there.
