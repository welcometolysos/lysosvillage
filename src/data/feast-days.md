## The Chryseleousa caveat

The Metropolis of Paphos page for this church states only that it keeps
**θεομητορικές εορτές** — feasts of the Mother of God — without naming a date.
The closely related Panagia Chryseleousa at Empa keeps **8 September**, the
Nativity of the Theotokos, not 15 August.

15 August is universally "της Παναγίας" in Cyprus and is when the diaspora
comes home, so it is a fair anchor for the Sacred Sky section. But it must not
be described as *this church's own panigyri* without the village saying so.

other website about Lysos can make.
